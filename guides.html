03 / KEYCARD SYSTEM

DNA WORKBENCH.

Community-supplied instructions for making and using the DNA workbench to remove, replace and program keycard chips.

IMPORTANT: Do not place the hologram before you have every item needed to fully build the workbench. The hologram can despawn.

01 · CRAFT THE KIT

  1. Combine 4 sticks and 1 metal wire to craft a workbench kit.
  2. Before placing it, obtain a soldering kit and a helping hands kit and put them in the kit's inventory.
  3. Place the hologram only once your full material collection is ready.

02 · BUILD THE BENCH

After placing the hologram, add:

  • 1 metal wire
  • 16 bolts
  • 5 metal sheets
  • 13 planks
  • 1 laptop
  • 1 card reader

Use a hammer to finish assembly. A hatchet can dismantle the finished bench or hologram.

03 · REMOVE AND INSTALL CHIPS

  1. Install a 40V battery in the bay at the top left of the workbench.
  2. With a used keycard in your hands, look at the helping hands and place the card there.
  3. Look at the soldering iron to remove the used chip.
  4. Open the top-left drawer and put solder and flux in its inventory along with the new chip.
  5. Use the soldering iron again to install the new chip.

04 · PROGRAM THE CARD

  1. Place the unprogrammed card into the card reader.
  2. Look at the laptop and select the programming option.
  3. Find chips in lootable blister packs: hold a pack in your hands and open it, then read its name or description for the chip color.

The supplied server instructions say supplies are found around the map rather than at traders. A built bench also has an in-game guide.

06 / ALL SURVIVORS

STARTER SURVIVAL TIPS.

STAY DRY AND WARM

Wet clothing and poor insulation can drain your temperature and stamina. Dry off, seek shelter, and use safe heat sources.

DRINK SAFELY

Prefer known safe water sources and purify questionable water. Avoid drinking untreated water that could make your character sick.

AVOID INFECTION

Keep bandages clean, pay attention to symptoms, and learn the modded treatment rules before relying on unfamiliar medicine.

PREPARE BEFORE BUILDING

Gather your full material list first and confirm server building restrictions, territory rules and despawn behavior with staff.

These are shared tips and community-reported mod instructions, not a declaration that every featured mod is installed on every PLAIN DAYZ server. Server-specific mod lists will be published as supplied.
