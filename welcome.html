01 / COMMUNITY

THE RULES.

Be the kind of survivor you'd want to meet. The rules below apply across our servers.

!
ZERO TOLERANCE: PvP & THEFT

Intentional player killing or stealing from another player—including their tombstone—will result in a permanent ban.

  1. Choose a real character name.

    Your in-game name must not be “Survivor.”

  2. No PvP, stealing, or looting tombstones.

    Do not attack, intentionally kill, or steal from other players or their tombstones. These offenses carry a permanent ban.

  3. Do not abuse vehicle reimbursements.

    If you sell a vehicle and falsely claim it despawned or fell into the water, your in-game bank balance will be reset to zero.

  4. Vehicle replacements are exceptional.

    Vehicles are only replaced on occasions such as staff error or a server lag/disconnection issue. Replacements are not guaranteed.

  5. Respect everyone.

    No harassment, hate speech, sexism, or hate directed at LGBTQ+ players.

  6. Open a support ticket for issues.

    Our team will respond as soon as possible. Please do not spam or repeatedly tag administrators.

  7. Don't take advantage of helpful players.

    Our community is generous. Treat other players' time, kindness, and belongings with respect.

  8. No loot cycling.

    Do not deliberately move unwanted loot to force new items to spawn.

  9. No cheating or exploits.

    Duping, hacking, and exploiting game bugs are prohibited.

  10. Respect vehicle ownership.

    Vehicles do not normally spawn around the map. If a vehicle is not yours, do not touch it.

02 / TERRITORIES

BASE BUILDING.

Build responsibly. Keep important locations clear and protect your territory.

MINIMUM BUILDING DISTANCES

1,000mFROM ANY TRADER
500mFROM MILITARY AREAS & CAMPS
200mFROM POLICE, HOSPITALS & FIRE STATIONS
⌁
KEEP PUBLIC ROUTES OPEN

Never block wells or roads, including building on top of or over a road.

MAXIMUM BASE SIZE — CHOOSE ONE

OPTION A4 × 4

Maximum 2 stories high

OPTION B2 × 2

Maximum 3 stories high

  1. One base per player.

    You may have only one base.

  2. Flagpole required.

    All bases must have a flagpole. Bases without one may be deleted.

  3. Secure your entrance.

    Every base must have a door or gate equipped with a code lock.

  4. Gardening limits apply.

    Maximum 3 greenhouses or 4 gardening plots at your base or elsewhere on the map.

  5. Do not raise somebody else's territory flag.

    Only interact with territory flags that belong to you or your group.

  6. Ask when you're unsure.

    If you aren't sure whether a build is permitted, open a Discord ticket before building.

  7. Move illegal bases within 24 hours.

    Illegal bases receive a warning in Discord and 24 hours to relocate. If it is your base, open a ticket. Bases not moved by the deadline will be deleted.

03 / GARAGE SYSTEM

VEHICLE RULES.

These rules cover cars, helicopters, and boats.

MAXIMUM OUT AT ONCE2

vehicles per player

When you're not actively using a vehicle, store it in a garage provided by the garage mod.

  1. Use garages for storage.

    All vehicles must be parked in a garage from the garage mod when not in use.

  2. Find or purchase a garage.

    The vehicle trader sells a garage kit you can place at your base. Public garages are available at traders, and additional public garage locations are marked on the in-game map.

  3. No unattended vehicles left in the world.

    Vehicles found around the map while their owners are offline will be deleted.

  4. No more than two vehicles outside at a time.

    Do not have more than two vehicles out of garage storage simultaneously.

  5. Store items at your own risk.

    Parking a vehicle with belongings in its inventory or attachment slots can result in those items disappearing. Lost items will not be refunded.

If something goes wrong, please open a ticket using the ticket tool in our Discord. That helps us track the problem and prevents requests from being forgotten. Please avoid repeatedly pinging staff.
